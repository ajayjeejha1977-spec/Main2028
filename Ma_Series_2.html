How to use this sheet

The seven groups below are formed by approach, not by topic, and are ordered from the highest miss rate to the lowest. Miss rates are my estimates from the circles and "vg" marks as I read them. The "Likely slips" in the drill are my judgement of typical errors, not student data.

Seen working well in the marks: direct AP, GP and HP formulas; infinite GP sums; standard Vieta with AP or HP roots; and setting up unknowns in classical AP/GP word problems.
