Logarithm — 8-Motif Remember Sheet

Theory from ALLEN Digital "Logarithm" (Parts 01–10) · Practice from Advance Problems in Mathematics, Class-XI (PT-1, PT-2, Ex-1 to Ex-4) · Stages 1–8 (motifs A, B, C, G, D, E, F, H in teaching order)

Stage 1 — A: Change of Base & Chaining S1PT1 Q1,8,9,10 · PT2 Q1 · Ex1 Q9,16,2a · Ex3 Q4,6,7,9 · ALLEN Pt 04, 08

1\(\log_b m=\dfrac{\log_a m}{\log_a b}\),   \(\log_b a=\dfrac1{\log_a b}\). Chains collapse: \(\log_a b\cdot\log_b c\cdot\log_c d=\log_a d\).
2\(\log_{a^k}m=\tfrac1k\log_a m\)   \(a^{\log_b c}=c^{\log_b a}\)   Antilog: \(\log_a n=m\iff n=a^m\).
3Given \(3^a=4,\,4^b=5,\dots,8^f=9\): write \(a=\log_34,\ b=\log_45,\dots\) ⇒ \(abcdef=\log_39=2\).
4To express \(\log_{140}63\) in given letters: prime-factorise (2,3,5,7), move everything to one base, then substitute.

Stage 2 — B: Core Identities & Proofs S2PT1 Q3,6 · PT2 Q8 · Ex1 Q2,17–19 · Ex2 Q11–13 · ALLEN Pt 02–04

5For \(m,n>0\): \(\log_a mn=\log_a m+\log_a n\),   \(\log_a\tfrac mn=\log_a m-\log_a n\),   \(\log_a m^x=x\log_a m\). Also \(a^{\log_aN}=N\), \(\log_a1=0\), \(\log_aa=1\).
6Telescoping: factorise into primes first (81/80, 25/24, 16/15) and watch the factors cancel.
7Cyclic trap: \(x^3+y^3+z^3=3xyz\) with \(x,y,z\) distinct ⇒ \(x+y+z=0\). With \(x=\log a\) etc. ⇒ \(abc=1\) Ex2 Q13
8Master identity: \(p=\log_abc,\ q=\log_bca,\ r=\log_cab\Rightarrow pqr=p+q+r+2\) returns in Ex3 Q11
9Irrationality proof: assume \(\log_23=p/q\Rightarrow2^p=3^q\) — even = odd, contradiction.

Stage 3 — C: Characteristic, Mantissa & Digits S3PT2 Q6,7 · Ex1 Q23–25 · Ex3 Q1,5 · ALLEN Pt 04(vi,vii)

10\(\log_{10}N=n+f\): characteristic \(n\) (integer), mantissa \(f\in[0,1)\). Digits of \(N\) = \(n+1\). Use \(\log2=0.3010,\ \log3=0.4771,\ \log5=1-\log2\).
11Digits in \(a^b\): \(\lfloor b\log a\rfloor+1\).   Zeros after the decimal point: if characteristic is \(-k\) there are \(k-1\) zeros. −14.894 = \(\bar{15}.106\) ⇒ 14 zeros
12Integers with characteristic \(n\) in base \(b\): \(b^n\le N<b^{n+1}\), count \(=b^{n+1}-b^n\).

Stage 4 — G: Domain & Inequalities S4PT1 Q7 · PT2 Q2,9,10 · ALLEN Pt 06–07 (graphs, Illus 1–6)

13Existence first: \(\log_aN\) needs \(N>0,\ a>0,\ a\ne1\). Write the domain before touching the inequality.
14\(a>1\): increasing, \(\log_ax<\log_ay\iff x<y\).   \(0<a<1\): decreasing, the inequality flips.   \(\log_ax>p\Rightarrow x>a^p\) (a>1) but \(0<x<a^p\) (a<1).
15Number and base on the same side of 1 ⇒ log positive; opposite sides ⇒ negative.
16Quadratic in \(u=\log x\) (ALLEN Illus 3): solve for \(u\), exponentiate, then intersect with the domain.

Stage 5 — D: Equations with Domain Vigilance S5PT1 Q5 · Ex1 Q3,13,20 · Ex2 Q1–8 · Ex3 Q3,4 · Ex4 Q4 · ALLEN Pt 05

174-step method: (1) domain, (2) one log per side or substitute \(t=\log x\), (3) solve, (4) reject roots outside the domain.
18\(\log_fg=k\Rightarrow g=f^k\) with \(f>0,f\ne1,g>0\). Variable base: \(\log_{x+1}(x^2+x-6)^2=4\) gives \(-7,-\tfrac52,1\); only \(x=1\) keeps the base valid.
19Squared argument: \(\log_{10}(x-6)^2=2\) ⇒ \(x=16\) and \(-4\); both valid. Test, never reject by habit.
20Different bases: \(\log_4(x-1)=\log_2(x-3)\Rightarrow x-1=(x-3)^2\), keep \(x>3\) ⇒ \(x=5\).

Stage 6 — E: Nested Logs S6PT1 Q4 · Ex1 Q5 · Ex2 Q6,14 · Ex3 Q2 · Ex4 Q2,3,6 · ALLEN Pt 02, 05

21Peel outside-in: \(\log_a(\log_bx)=c\Rightarrow\log_bx=a^c\Rightarrow x=b^{a^c}\). Every layer's argument must stay positive.
22\(\log_a(\cdot)=0\Rightarrow(\cdot)=1\). Four layers: \(1+\log_pN=1\Rightarrow N=1\).   Base \(<1\): \(\log_{1/2}(\cdot)=1\Rightarrow(\cdot)=\tfrac12\).
23Heavy case: let \(t=\log_2x>0\), combine \(2\log_2t-\log_2(\tfrac32+t)=1\) ⇒ \(t^2-2t-3=0\) ⇒ \(t=3\), \(x=8\).

Stage 7 — F: Systems of Log Equations S7Ex1 Q21,22 · Ex2 Q9,10 · Ex3 Q11,12 · Ex4 Q1,5,10

24Rename \(u=\log x,\ v=\log y,\ w=\log z\): the system is ordinary algebra; convert back at the end.
25Symmetric sums: \(u^2+v^2+w^2=S^2-2P\) where \(S=u+v+w,\ P=uv+vw+wu\) Ex3 Q11: \(81^2-936=5625\)
26\(\log_yx+\log_xy=t+\tfrac1t\) with \(t=\log_yx\).   Mixed bases ⇒ convert to base 2 first (Ex2 Q9: \(\tfrac u3+v=5,\ \tfrac v3+u=7\)).

Stage 8 — H: Exponent–Log Hybrids S8PT1 Q2 · PT2 Q3 · Ex1 Q2b,11,15 · Ex2 Q1,2,5 · Ex3 Q10 · Ex4 Q5,9 · ALLEN Pt 04, 09

27Take log of both sides: \(x^{\log x}=\dfrac{10^4}{x^3}\Rightarrow(\log x)^2+3\log x-4=0\). Product of roots \(=10^{\text{sum of }\log\text{-roots}}=10^{-3}\).
28\((a^{\log_bc})^{\log_bc}\): use \(a^{\log_aN}=N\).   \(k^{\log_25}=16\Rightarrow k^{(\log_25)^2}=16^{\log_25}=5^4=625\).
29\(81^{1/\log_53}=81^{\log_35}=3^{4\log_35}=5^4\).   \(49^{1-\log_72}=\tfrac{49}4,\ 5^{-\log_54}=\tfrac14\). Convert every power to a common prime base.
